## Notes

- These models were generated by an AI agent using Autodesk Fusion.
- The primary modeling operations are sketches and extrusions.
- Some construction planes, patterns, fillets, and chamfers are also included.
- Sketches may be under-constrained.
- These models are provided for demonstration and educational purposes.
- Mechanical operation, interference, strength, tolerances, and manufacturability have not been fully validated.
